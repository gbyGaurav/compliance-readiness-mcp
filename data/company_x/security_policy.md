# Company X Information Security Governance Policy

**Document ID:** CX-POL-SEC-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-15  
**Owner:** Information Security Office, Company X (Fictional Customer)

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes baseline security governance for Company X's corporate environments, IT assets, cloud services, and employee operations. It applies to all personnel, contractors, and third-party systems handling Company X intellectual property and business data.

## 2. Security Roles and Responsibilities
- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO):** Accountable for enterprise risk management, security posture, and compliance validation.
- **Security Engineering Team:** Responsible for implementing perimeter defenses, vulnerability scanners, and continuous monitoring controls.
- **System Owners:** Responsible for maintaining system configurations aligned with approved baseline standards.
- **Employees and Contractors:** Required to observe acceptable use standards and report suspicious activities immediately.

## 3. Risk Management and Compliance
Company X conducts biannual security risk assessments across all tier-1 infrastructure. Any identified risks exceeding acceptable thresholds must be entered into the enterprise risk register and remediated within defined service level agreements (SLAs).

## 4. Vendor and Third-Party Risk Management
Before onboarding external SaaS or infrastructure providers, vendor security assessments must be performed. Vendors must demonstrate compliance with industry standards (e.g., SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001) and agree to Company X's standard data protection addendum.

## 5. Security Training and Awareness
All employees must complete mandatory security awareness training within 30 days of hiring and annually thereafter. Training covers phishing defense, credential hygiene, and incident reporting procedures.
