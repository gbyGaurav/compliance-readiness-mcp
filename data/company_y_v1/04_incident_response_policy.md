# Company Y - Incident Response Policy

**Version:** 1.0

Company Y maintains an incident response process with defined responsibilities for Security, Infrastructure, and Engineering teams.

The process includes identification, containment, investigation, recovery, and post-incident review.

Security incidents involving sensitive systems must be escalated to the incident response team.

The company targets notification of relevant stakeholders within 48 hours of confirming a significant security incident.

The Incident Response Lead coordinates major incidents.
