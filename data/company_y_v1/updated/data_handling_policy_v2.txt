COMPANY Y
Data Handling & Residency Policy
Version: 2.0

Company Y stores production data in cloud infrastructure.
All customer training data, model checkpoints, inference prompts, and output vectors must reside exclusively within US sovereign cloud regions (AWS GovCloud (US) or Azure Government).
Replication and disaster recovery must stay within US sovereign regions. Cross-border replication and multi-region fallback outside the continental US are prohibited.
Remote access from non-US IP addresses and transit through non-US networks are prohibited.
Sensitive workloads continue to use encryption and access controls.
Exceptions require written approval from the Security Team.

Owner: Infrastructure Team
Review: Annual
