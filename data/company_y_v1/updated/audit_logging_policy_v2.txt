COMPANY Y
Audit Logging Policy
Version: 2.0

Company Y collects authentication, application, and infrastructure logs.
AI inference activity (queries, model/version used, requesting identity) and administrative changes must also be logged.
Logs are written to tamper-evident, append-only WORM storage (e.g., S3 Object Lock in Compliance mode) with integrity hashes.
Logs are retained for a minimum of 365 days.
Logs are forwarded to the centralized SIEM in real time and reviewed by the Security Team for investigations.

Owner: Infrastructure and Security Teams
Review: Annual
