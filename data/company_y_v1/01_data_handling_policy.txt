COMPANY Y
Data Handling & Residency Policy
Version: 1.0

Company Y stores production data in cloud infrastructure. Production data may be replicated across multiple global regions for availability and disaster recovery.

Approved cloud services may transfer data between regions when required for operational resilience.

Sensitive workloads use encryption and access controls, but this policy does not require a US-only geographic boundary or prohibit international transit.

Owner: Infrastructure Team
Review: Annual
